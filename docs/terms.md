# Terms of use — Nova Glucose

*Last updated: 1 November 2026*

1. Nova Glucose is a private family tool provided as is, without warranty.
2. It is **not a medical device**. Readings, alerts, food ideas and photo carb estimates are for information only and can be

3. The app **does not calculate or suggest insulin doses**. Insulin decisions remain with the family and their doctor.
4. The official FreeStyle Libre / LibreLinkUp apps and their alarms should stay active.
5. Data stays with the user (phone, Download folder, the user's own Google Drive). See the [privacy policy](privacy.html).
