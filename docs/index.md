# Nova Glucose

Nova Glucose is a private Android app made by a parent for his family. It follows a FreeStyle Libre sensor through
LibreLinkUp, shows glucose readings and charts, raises high and low alerts, suggests food ideas, estimates carbohydrates
from meal photos, and keeps a logbook of meals and insulin doses entered by the family.

It does not calculate or suggest insulin doses.
